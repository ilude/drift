# The Lost Fleet: Weapons Technology Reference

> Source: Jack Campbell's "The Lost Fleet" series. User-requested research for weapons tech design. Agent-generated summary.

## Weapon Types

### Hell-Lances (Charged Particle Beams)
- Primary weapon of both Alliance and Syndicate warships
- Fires charged particle "spears" at high speed
- Does NOT consume expendable ammunition — requires only ship power
- Medium effective range (shorter than missiles, longer than null-fields)
- Targeting during fleet engagements handled by ship AI

### Grapeshot (Magnetic Railgun Projectiles)
- Metal ball bearings fired from magnetic railguns
- Primary role: deplete enemy shields before hell-lance engagement
- Secondary role: intercept incoming missiles
- Expendable ammunition, but easy/cheap to manufacture from common materials
- Effective range: ~0.5 to 1.0 light-seconds (150,000-300,000 km)
- Reload time: at least 5 seconds between salvos
- Typically fired twice per engagement pass: once at extreme range, again just before hell-lance range

### Specter Missiles (Guided Munitions)
- Autonomous guided missiles with multiple warheads
- Longest effective range of any ship-to-ship weapon
- More maneuverable than ships — can out-accelerate warships
- Expendable, difficult/expensive to manufacture compared to grapeshot
- A critical logistics constraint: fleet was at one point down to 41 specters total

### Null-Field Projectors
- Alliance-exclusive technology
- Produces a massive energy ball that temporarily nullifies the strong nuclear force
- Matter hit by a null field literally falls apart at the atomic level
- Very limited range — essentially a close-range super-weapon
- Cannot be used within a major gravity well
- Can be stopped by strong shields

### Kinetic Projectiles ("Rocks")
- Large chunks of metal accelerated to high speed
- Devastating against planetary surfaces and static installations
- Useless in ship-to-ship combat — too slow/predictable

## Weapon vs. Defense Interactions

**Shields:** First line of defense. Bow shields always strongest. Grapeshot specifically designed to deplete shields. Null fields can be stopped by strong shields.

**Armor:** Second line after shields fail. Varies by ship class — battleships heaviest, battle cruisers lighter (traded for speed), cruisers/destroyers minimal.

**Point Defenses:** Active systems targeting incoming missiles. Can be overwhelmed by large missile salvos.

**Engagement Layering:**
1. Point defenses engage incoming specters at long range
2. Grapeshot salvos strip shields during approach
3. Hell-lances penetrate to armor/hull once shields down
4. Null fields disintegrate whatever remains at close range

## Weapon Triangle

| Dimension | Hell-Lances (Energy) | Grapeshot (Kinetic) | Specters (Missiles) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ammo** | Unlimited (power-based) | Expendable but cheap | Expendable and expensive |
| **Range** | Medium | Medium-long | Long |
| **Role** | Primary damage dealer | Shield stripping, missile defense | Opening salvos, pursuit shots |
| **Logistics** | No supply concern | Low supply concern | Critical supply constraint |

## Combat Physics

- Fleets close at ~0.2c, combined closing speed up to 0.4c
- Actual firing window: fractions of a second to a few seconds
- Battle = multiple passes with long repositioning intervals
- Fire control AI handles targeting — human reaction time insufficient
- Sensor data always stale due to light-speed lag
- Fuel cells limited to ~10 acceleration/deceleration cycles

## Game Design Takeaways

1. **Layered engagement ranges** force sequenced weapon use
2. **Expendable vs. unlimited ammo** creates logistics dimension
3. **Engagement geometry** matters (shield facing, weapon arcs, closing speed)
4. **Pass structure** creates discrete decision points
5. **Light-lag** makes positioning and prediction primary skills

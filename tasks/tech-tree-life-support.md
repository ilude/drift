# Tech Tree: Life Support & Crew (Brennan/Tanaka Cross-Domain)

> Status: Draft design (2026-03-28). Needs Aurora-scale expansion. Parent doc: [tech-tree-overview.md](tech-tree-overview.md)

Ship habitability, crew endurance, and the biological side of spacefaring. Where the Brennan exploration ethos meets Tanaka's pragmatic logistics.

---

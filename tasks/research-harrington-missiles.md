# Honor Harrington: Missile & Sensor/EW Technology Reference

> Source: David Weber's Honor Harrington series (Honorverse). User-requested research for missile tech progression and sensor work. Agent-generated summary.

## Missile Tech Generations

### Gen 0 — Contact Nuclear Warheads (pre-1800 PD)
- Megaton-yield fusion, requires near-contact detonation. Useless against armored ships with PD.

### Gen 1 — Laser Head Warheads (~1800-1860 PD)
- Pivotal innovation. Gravity generators focus nuclear blast into Gaussian cone aimed at lasing rods.
- Rods eject before detonation, each with own thrusters and sensors for alignment.
- Effective damage at ~25,000 km standoff. Larger missiles = longer rods = greater standoff.
- **This is what makes missiles lethal** — without it, energy weapons dominate forever.

### Gen 2 — Single-Drive Impeller Missiles (~1900 PD)
- Standard: ~80 tons, 46,000g accel, 180-second burn. Powered range: ~6 million km.
- Missiles can hurt ships but can't kill without overwhelming numbers. Energy weapons still decisive.

### Gen 3 — Multi-Drive Missiles (MDMs) (~1910-1915 PD)
- Multiple sequential single-use drives. Manticoran 3-stage Mark 23: ~30 million km range (~1.7 light-min).
- Havenite 2-stage: capacitor-powered, cruder copy.
- **Range is power.** The side with MDMs wins until the other side catches up, restoring parity at new range.

### Gen 4 — Apollo System (~1920+ PD)
- Each pod: 8 Mark 23 MDMs + 1 Mark 23-E control missile with FTL telemetry link.
- Keyhole II platform communicates FTL to control missile, which relays to its 8-missile clutch.
- Result: real-time fire control at ~3 light-minutes (54 million km). Eliminates light-speed lag degradation.
- **The "software upgrade" that obsoletes hardware parity** — same missiles, dramatically better accuracy.

### Gen 5 — Mesan Alignment Weapons (~1922 PD)
- **Graser Torpedo:** Autonomous vessel with single-shot cruiser-grade graser. Spider-drive (undetectable by gravitic sensors). Beam lasts 3 seconds vs milliseconds for laser head — ~100x delivered energy.
- **Cataphract/Ninurta:** Attempts to duplicate MDMs using different tech base.

## Warhead Types
- **Contact fusion:** Megaton-yield, near-contact. Obsolete. Haven revived as "Triple Ripple" — three timed nuclear volleys to overwhelm sensors.
- **Bomb-pumped laser head:** The standard. Grav-focused detonation drives X-ray laser rods.
- **Graser warhead:** 3-second sustained graser beam, ~100x laser head energy. Mesan only.
- **ECM warhead:** Dazzlers, Dragon's Teeth, Apollo control missiles.

## Missile Defense Layers

1. **Passive (always on):** Impeller wedge (impenetrable top/bottom), sidewalls (port/starboard), armor. Critical vulnerability: bow/stern gaps.
2. **Counter-missiles (1-4 million km):** Destroy incoming by overlapping impeller wedges. Mark 31: 130,000g, 3.6M km range. Keyhole platforms extend coordination.
3. **Point defense lasers (terminal):** 8 emitters per cluster, 1 shot every 2 seconds. Very short engagement window.
4. **Electronic warfare:** Shipboard jammers, Ghost Rider drones, Dazzlers, Dragon's Teeth, Lorelei decoys.
5. **System defense networks:** Moriarty (Haven), Mycroft (Grand Alliance), Testudo/Scutum (Mesan) — centralized fire-control platforms.

## The Pod Revolution

**Before pods:** Ships fired from fixed broadside tubes. Hull size = magazine depth = firepower. Stalemate.

**SD(P) / Podnought:** Hollow-core superdreadnought storing 500+ pods internally. Rate: 300 missiles/minute, shoots dry in ~17 minutes.

**Doctrinal impact:** First salvos become decisive. Alpha-strike vs sustained-fire creates a genuine ship class split. Fleet positioning and timing matter more than sustained slugging.

## Sensors & Electronic Warfare

### Sensor Types
- **Gravitic Sensors (passive, FTL):** Detect impeller wedges in real-time at enormous range. Backbone of long-range detection. Ship with drives shut down is invisible to gravitics.
- **Radar/Lidar (active, lightspeed):** Short-range detection and fire control. Even with best stealth, ships detectable at ~800,000 km.
- **Passive EM:** Detect emissions from active sensors, comms, drives.

### Visibility Thresholds
1. **Wedge active:** Visible to gravitics at unlimited range, in real-time (FTL)
2. **Wedge down, systems active:** Invisible to gravitics. Detectable by passive EM at medium range
3. **Zero emissions, coasting:** Only detectable by active radar/lidar at ~800,000 km
4. **Spider drive (Mesan):** Undetectable by gravitics while still maneuvering. The stealth breakthrough.

### EW Progression
- **Early:** Built-in jammers, tethered decoy drones (power beamed from ship)
- **Ghost Rider:** Untethered decoy drones, improved seeker heads, Dazzlers (fusion-powered jamming missiles), Dragon's Teeth (decoy missiles simulating full volleys)
- **Lorelei:** Independent decoys mimicking stealthed warships at fleet-level distances
- **Triple Ripple (Haven):** Three timed nuclear volleys to overwhelm/blind Manticoran sensors. Brute force counters finesse.

### Recon Drones
- ~270 tons, deployed from boat bays. Extended sensor range as remote platforms.
- FTL-capable variants with ~4 light-hour range. Ghost Rider drones with micro-fusion plants, stealth, FTL comms.

## Game Design Patterns

1. **Measure-countermeasure spiral:** Every offensive gen spawns a defensive response and vice versa
2. **Alpha strike vs sustained fire:** Pod-layers are glass cannons — more first-salvo firepower but weaker hull, finite ammo
3. **Sensor range as asymmetric advantage:** FTL detection + light-speed fire control creates a gap that Apollo collapses
4. **Stealth as binary tradeoff:** Invisible = vulnerable (no wedge/shields). Spider drive breaks this binary — most disruptive tech.
5. **EW as salvo component:** Dazzlers/Dragon's Teeth compete for magazine slots with actual warheads
6. **Platform externalization:** Keyhole, Testudo, Lorelei — trend toward externalizing capabilities onto deployable platforms

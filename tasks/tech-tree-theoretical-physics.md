# Tech Tree: Theoretical Physics (Sakarov Domain)

> Status: Draft design (2026-03-28). Needs Aurora-scale expansion. Parent doc: [tech-tree-overview.md](tech-tree-overview.md)

Pure research that doesn't directly produce ship components but unlocks branches across all other domains. The Sakarov Institute's legacy — foundational breakthroughs that others build on. This is the "root system" of the entire tech tree.

Additional theoretical branches: TN Thermodynamics (how TN fields interact with heat — feeds into reactor and stealth tech), TN Particle Physics (substructure of TNEs — feeds into materials and weapons), TN Information Theory (theoretical basis for TN computing), TN Biology (theoretical basis for Vendarite applications). Each should be a prerequisite cluster that unlocks practical tech across multiple domains.

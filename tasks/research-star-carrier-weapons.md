# Star Carrier / Xul: Weapons & Carrier Doctrine Reference

> Source: Ian Douglas's Star Carrier series and Heritage/Legacy/Inheritance trilogies. User-requested research for carrier/fighter doctrine and asymmetric warfare. Agent-generated summary.

## Human Weapon Types

### Kinetic Weapons
- **Gatling railguns:** Standard fighter armament. Steel-jacketed depleted uranium slugs at relativistic speeds.
- **Spinal railguns:** Capital ship weapons. Carrier *America* has spinal-mount railguns.
- **Launch catapult repurposing:** Fighter launch catapults can accelerate kinetic slugs instead — dual-purpose infrastructure.
- **KAM pellets:** Kinetic-kill Anti-Missile projectiles. Fighter-launched point defense.

### Particle Beams
- **PBP (Particle Beam Projector):** Fighter-mounted (StellarDyne Blue Lightning PBP-2). Capital ship spinal-mount rated at 1.15 TeV, fire rate 1/15 seconds. Particles accelerated to near-c.

### Nuclear Missiles
- **Krait:** Fighter-launched, nuclear-tipped. Devastating at relativistic launch speeds.
- **Boomslang, Taipan, Mamba:** Progressively upgraded variants. Venomous snake naming convention.
- AI-guided with multi-spectrum tracking (heat, radar, mass, gravitometric, X-ray, neutrino, optical). No effective way to jam all incoming simultaneously.

### Sand Cannons / AMSO
- **AMSO canisters (Anti-Missile Sand/Ordnance):** Defensive countermeasures releasing clouds of refractive particles. Defeat laser targeting, physically intercept threats. **Key innovation:** Trevor Gray fires AMSO at near-light-speed offensively — turning defense into a devastating relativistic weapon. Emergent dual-use from consistent rules.

### Anti-Matter Weapons
- Exist but **strictly forbidden** by Confederate law. When used against USNA Marines, President declares war. Political/narrative device — technically available, severe consequences.

### Exotic Weapons (Alien)
- **Soru:** Gamma ray lasers
- **Sh'daar Attack Drones:** Meson beams carrying nuclear strong force — targets undergo nuclear collapse, simply disappear
- **Slan:** Fusion and positron beams
- **H'rulka:** Fire miniature black holes. One-shot smaller ships at range.
- **Nungiirtok:** Gravity weapons that crush targets

## Fighter / Strike Craft Technology

### SG-92 Starhawk
- **Mass:** 22 metric tons
- **Drive:** Singularity drive — creates artificial gravitational singularity ahead of craft. 50,000g acceleration. Zero to 0.997c in 10 minutes.
- **Variable geometry:** Nanomatrix hull allows radical shape changes. Needle for FTL transit, swept-wing for atmosphere, Y-shape with weapons pods for combat.
- **Armament:** Gatling railguns, PBP-2 particle beam, dozens of nuclear missiles, sandcasters, KAM pellets, spoofer decoys
- **Self-repair:** Nanomatrix hull repairs battle damage during combat. Limited by energy throughput — can be overwhelmed by sustained fire.
- **AI integration:** Onboard AI handles targeting, munitions launch (pilot confirms), evasive maneuvers. AI faster than human but at extreme velocities even AI reaction time insufficient.

### Key Fighter Doctrine
- **High-c strike:** Fleet arrives ~40 AU out, immediately launches fighters. Fighters reach near-c in 10 min. Light warning from arrival gives enemy ~5 minutes before fighters hit — almost zero reaction time.
- **One fighter vs capital ships:** A single fighter at relativistic speed can wreck multiple warships by launching nuclear-tipped missiles during approach.
- **Size threshold:** Ships <80m use singularity drives (extreme accel). Ships >80m must use Alcubierre-type FTL (lower accel, ~50g). Hard class boundary in physics.

## Carrier Operations

### CVS *America*
- **Shape:** "Titanic mushroom" — km-long stem with hemispheric cap (reaction mass + radiation shield)
- **Hab rings:** Twin counter-rotating. Fighter launch tubes use rotational g-forces (0.5g) for initial acceleration.
- **Fighter complement:** 6 squadrons standard, up to 8 wartime
- **Own weapons:** Spinal PBP, spinal railguns, AI-controlled point defense. These are **last-resort self-defense only** — the carrier is NOT a combatant.

### Carrier Doctrine
- **Carrier is weak alone.** "Vulnerable, slow, nowhere nearly powerful enough to go head to head with a destroyer." Her role is entirely deploying/recovering the fighter wing.
- **Fleet composition (CBG-18):** ~27 ships: carrier, heavy cruisers, 1 battleship, 4 destroyers, ~6 frigates, supply/repair, 8 transports. Only 6 carriers of this class existed.
- **Shield dilemma:** Gravitic shields block ALL EM including light and comms. Carrier under heavy fire cannot shoot back, communicate, or coordinate. Must rely on escorts to suppress fire enough to drop shields and operate.

## Defense Systems

### Gravitic Shields
- Manipulate spacetime to bend incoming ordnance back. Missiles destroyed, beams deflected.
- **Also blocks all light, comms, and sensor data.** Must be dropped to fire weapons, communicate, or receive sensor data.
- Two 10-kiloton detonations at close range can leak energy through.
- **Core game mechanic:** Shields are extremely effective but impose total blackout. When to drop shields to return fire vs staying shielded = genuine decision space.

### Nanomatrix Hull Self-Repair
- Nano-laminates dissolve, recombine, reshape on command. Real-time combat repair.
- **Rate-limited** — can be overwhelmed by sustained high-energy impacts (fusion beams at fractional c).
- Also enables variable-geometry capability.

### Point Defense
- KAM pellets (~10km intercept), AMSO sand clouds, AI-controlled PD weapons, spoofer decoys.

## Xul Technology (Heritage/Legacy/Inheritance)

### Scale
- Dominated galaxy for **10 million years**. Tech "at least a thousand years ahead" — possibly millions.
- **Hunterships:** Flattened spheroids, 2-20 km across. Travel at 0.95c.
- Human comparison: fleet's ~200 starships mostly mass under 12,000 tons. Against Xul km-scale ships, "minnows nibbling at whales."

### Xul Weapons
- **Asteroid bombardment:** Primary offensive. Attack on Earth killed ~4 billion.
- **Patterning:** Devours vessels and transfers humans/AIs as living, self-aware programs into Xul computer banks. Victims eternally conscious.
- **Reality-rewriting weapon:** Makes targets simply cease to exist.

### How Humanity Counters (Asymmetric Warfare)
- **Stargate network:** Ancient portal system for surprise attacks and rapid redeployment.
- **Nova weapons:** Cause stars to go nova, destroying Xul bases. Marines steer a star into galactic core black hole.
- **Boarding actions:** Infantry-scale combat inside alien megastructures.
- **Speed and evasion:** Can't match firepower, so stay beneath notice and strike targets of opportunity.

## Game Design Takeaways

1. **Shield/comms tradeoff:** Shields block everything including your own sensors. Genuine decision space.
2. **Carrier doctrine:** Carrier projects power through fighters, weak alone. Creates fleet composition depth.
3. **Size-based drive threshold:** Hard class boundary — fighters use one physics, capitals use another.
4. **AMSO dual-use:** Defensive system repurposed offensively. Emergent from consistent rules.
5. **Nanomatrix repair vs damage throughput:** Self-repair has a rate limit. Concentrated fire overwhelms it.
6. **AI autonomy spectrum:** From fighter targeting assist to strategic super-intelligence.
7. **Asymmetric warfare:** Small forces using mobility, stargates, and superweapons against vast superiority.
8. **Weapon diversity per faction:** Each alien has distinct weapon type — variety of threat profiles.
